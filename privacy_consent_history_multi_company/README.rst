This module makes the consent log multi-company. It is a bridge between
*Privacy Consent History* and *Privacy Consent Multi Company*, installed
automatically when both are present:

-  every row of the consent log belongs to the company of its data
   processing activity at the time the answer was recorded; the company
   is kept as evidence even if the activity is later moved to another
   company;
-  archived rows keep the company of the log row they come from;
-  users only see the log and archive rows of the companies selected in
   the company switcher. Rows archived before this module was installed
   have no company and are visible in every company.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_

Usage
=====

1. Open the consent log, or its archive, from the reporting menu of
   *Data Protection*.
2. Use the company switcher to choose which companies' rows are shown,
   and group the rows by *Company* when more than one company is
   selected.
