This module makes privacy consents multi-company. It is a bridge between
*Privacy - Consent* and *Privacy Multi Company*, installed automatically when
both are present:

- every consent belongs to the company of its data processing activity;
- users only see the consents of the companies selected in the company
  switcher, also in the consents smart button of contacts;
- consent requests are generated and sent one company at a time, also by the
  daily scheduled action;
- when the company has a privacy email, consent request emails are sent from
  that address, which is also the reply-to address.
