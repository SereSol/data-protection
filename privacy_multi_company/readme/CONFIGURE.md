To set the privacy email of a company:

1.  Go to *Settings \> Companies* and open the company.
2.  Fill in *Privacy Email*, for example the address of the data protection
    officer.

Activities that exist when the module is installed are assigned to the
company whose contact is their controller; when no company matches, they go
to the main company and their controller becomes its contact, with a note in
the activity's chatter. Check those activities after installation.
