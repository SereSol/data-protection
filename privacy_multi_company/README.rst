This module makes data processing activities multi-company, for
databases where each company is a separate data controller:

-  every activity belongs to exactly one company;
-  the controller of an activity is the contact of its company,
   read-only in the form view;
-  users only see the activities of the companies selected in the
   company switcher;
-  each company can set a privacy email, the address for data protection
   matters that other privacy modules can use as sender and contact.

Consents are made multi-company by a separate bridge module.

   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.

Configuration
=============

To set the privacy email of a company:

1. Go to *Settings > Companies* and open the company.
2. Fill in *Privacy Email*, for example the address of the data
   protection officer.

Activities that exist when the module is installed are assigned to the
company whose contact is their controller; when no company matches, they
go to the main company and their controller becomes its contact, with a
note in the activity's chatter. Check those activities after
installation.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Privacy > Master Data > Activities* and create an activity.
2. Choose its *Company*: it defaults to the current company. The
   *Controller* is filled with the company's contact and cannot be
   changed.
3. Use the company switcher to choose which companies' activities are
   shown.
