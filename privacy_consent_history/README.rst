This module keeps evidence of every consent answer:

-  a log row for each answer, with date and time of the server, IP
   address, browser, who filled it in and as whom (data subject, parent,
   legal guardian, employee), source (back office, email link, import)
   and an optional related document;
-  when an employee changes the acceptance of a consent in the back
   office, the consent becomes *Answered* and the employee must say how
   and when the answer was collected (configurable collection methods,
   collection date, note and attachments, posted in the chatter);
-  the log cannot be changed or deleted by users;
-  an archive keeps a copy of the log detached from the contact, for
   example before the contact is anonymized, and deletes it
   automatically after the retention period set in the settings.

Other modules can extend the sources and roles of the log and confirm
answers given on a page only when the page is submitted.

Configuration
=============

1. Go to *Privacy > Configuration > Collection Methods* to edit how
   consents can be collected in the back office, and whether each method
   requires a note.
2. Go to *Privacy > Settings* (or *Settings > Data Protection*) and set
   the *Archive retention* in years. With 0 the archive is kept
   indefinitely.

Usage
=====

To record an answer collected by the office:

1. Open a consent and change *Accepted*.
2. The section *How it was collected* unlocks: choose the collection
   method, the collection date and, if needed, a note and attachments.
3. Save: the consent is answered, the chatter shows how the answer was
   collected and the *Answer log* gets a new row.

The full log is in *Privacy > Reports > Consent Log*, the archive in
*Privacy > Reports > Consent Log Archive*.

Developers can call ``res.partner._privacy_consent_log_archive()`` to
move the log of a contact to the archive, for example before anonymizing
it.
